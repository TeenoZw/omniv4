# Data Subject Request Procedure

## Purpose
To ensure requests for access, correction, deletion, objection, withdrawal of consent, and related privacy rights are logged, verified, assessed, and resolved consistently.

## Intake channels
- Email: info@omnilogistics.co.zw
- Any approved future ticketing or portal workflow

## Request types
- access request
- correction / update request
- deletion / destruction / de-identification request
- objection to processing
- withdrawal of consent
- complaint

## Minimum intake information
- full name
- contact details
- relationship to the data subject if acting on their behalf
- sufficient detail to identify the record or information
- proof of identity or authority where needed

## Handling steps
1. Log the request in the request register.
2. Acknowledge receipt.
3. Verify identity and authority.
4. Assess the legal basis, scope, and systems affected.
5. Gather responsive records or determine lawful limits.
6. Communicate the outcome and retain the response record.

## Decision guidance
- If the request is unclear, ask for clarification before proceeding.
- If the request relates to personal information, consider both POPIA and any contract or operational limitations.
- If the request is effectively a PAIA request for records, record that classification in the register.
- If disclosure would affect a third party, legally privileged content, or protected commercial information, escalate for review before responding.

## Internal roles
- Information Officer: oversight, escalation, and approval of difficult or sensitive responses.
- Operations / administration: intake, logging, and coordination.
- Technical owner: retrieval of system logs, user records, or platform evidence where needed.
- Finance / commercial owner: retrieval of invoices, quotations, and commercial records where relevant.

## Closure requirements
Before closing a request, confirm that the register contains:
- the final response date;
- the decision taken;
- the legal or operational basis for the decision;
- a copy of the response; and
- the handler and approver details.

## Registers to maintain
- request reference number
- request type
- date received
- data subject name
- verifier / handler
- due date
- outcome
- date closed
- notes / legal basis
