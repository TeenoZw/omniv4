# 30 - Admin Onboarding & Customer Lifecycle

This document describes the **new Omni Logistics onboarding workflow** aligned to the Wialon partnership. Omni Logistics manages onboarding, billing, and support, while tracking access is provided through Wialon.

---

## 1. Enquiry Intake

**Actors:** Customer, Omni Admin  
**Purpose:** Collect a detailed enquiry that leads into onboarding.

**Steps:**

1. Customer submits the enquiry form with profile, hardware choices, and add-ons.
2. Admin reviews the request, checks fleet size and operating area, and assigns an owner.
3. Status stays at `new` until a quote is sent.

---

## 2. Quotation & Approval

**Actors:** Omni Admin, Finance  
**Purpose:** Respond with pricing and confirm readiness to onboard.

**Steps:**

1. Admin prepares a hardware + plan quotation.
2. Quotation is sent to the customer via email.
3. Admin updates status to `quoted` and then `approved` once the customer accepts.

---

## 3. Hub & User Provisioning

**Actors:** Omni Admin  
**Purpose:** Create the customer workspace and primary access.

**Steps:**

1. Create the hub in the Admin Portal (individual or business).
2. Assign plan type (Individual or Business) and billing cadence (monthly).
3. Create the primary user account and link it to the hub.
4. Share the hub code and Omni Eye Portal login instructions with the customer.

---

## 4. Hardware & Installation

**Actors:** Omni Admin, Technician  
**Purpose:** Provision and activate hardware for tracking.

**Steps:**

1. Reserve hardware from inventory and confirm IMEI/serial.
2. Assign a technician job card if installation is required.
3. Confirm installation completion and device activation.

---

## 5. Billing & Subscription Activation

**Actors:** Finance, Omni Admin  
**Purpose:** Start billing and subscription management.

**Steps:**

1. Create billing records and issue the first invoice.
2. Confirm payment status and activate the subscription.
3. Schedule recurring monthly billing and renewals.

---

## 6. Wialon Handoff

**Actors:** Omni Admin, Customer  
**Purpose:** Provide live tracking access through Wialon.

**Steps:**

1. Share the Wialon access link and credentials.
2. Validate login with a sample device if needed.
3. Confirm the customer can view assets in Wialon.

---

## 7. Support & Feedback

**Actors:** Customer, Support  
**Purpose:** Maintain customer success after onboarding.

**Steps:**

1. Support receives requests via email/phone/WhatsApp.
2. Tickets are logged and assigned to the support team.
3. Feedback is collected after resolution.

---

## Checklist (Admin)

- [ ] Enquiry reviewed and assigned.
- [ ] Quotation sent and approved.
- [ ] Hub created with plan type (Individual or Business).
- [ ] Hardware provisioned or installation scheduled.
- [ ] Billing activated and subscription set to monthly.
- [ ] Wialon login confirmed.
- [ ] Support channel communicated to customer.
