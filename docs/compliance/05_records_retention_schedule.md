# Starter Records Retention Schedule

This is a working schedule and requires legal review.

| Record category | Example records | Proposed retention basis | Proposed action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate registration | CIPC certificates, incorporation documents | retain while active and thereafter as legally required | retain |
| Information Officer records | registration certificate, appointment records | retain while active and thereafter as legally required | retain |
| Client contracts | agreements, onboarding records | contract term plus required legal period | review then retain/destroy |
| Billing records | invoices, receipts, subscription changes | tax and accounting requirements | retain then destroy |
| Audit logs | admin activity logs | operational security / accountability requirement | retain per approved schedule |
| Device / asset records | installations, assignments, recalls | service delivery and accountability requirement | review then retain/destroy |
| Support records | support tickets, incident notes | operational requirement | review then retain/destroy |
| Data subject requests | request register and correspondence | compliance evidence requirement | retain then review |
| Security incidents | incident register and investigation records | compliance and security requirement | retain then review |
