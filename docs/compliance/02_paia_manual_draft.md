# Draft PAIA Manual

Status: internal working draft for completion, approval, and legal review before publication or formal reliance.

## 1. Introduction
This manual is prepared for Omni Logistics ZW (Pty) Ltd to support access-to-information handling under the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000 ("PAIA"), read together with POPIA and related governance obligations.

This draft is intended to:
- identify the broad classes of records held by the organisation;
- set out how requests should be submitted and handled;
- clarify internal accountability for access-to-information matters; and
- create a base document that can be finalised with legal and operational review.

## 3. Purpose of this manual
The purpose of this manual is to describe:
- how requesters may request access to records held by Omni Logistics ZW (Pty) Ltd;
- the categories of records that may be available;
- the contact point for requests and related enquiries; and
- the broad internal process for handling requests lawfully and consistently.

## 4. Availability of the manual
Once approved, the final manual should be:
- published on the public website;
- maintained by the Information Officer;
- reviewed at least annually; and
- updated when material legal, structural, or operational changes occur.

## 5. Information Officer
The Information Officer is responsible for:
- oversight of PAIA and POPIA access-related compliance;
- receiving or supervising requests;
- coordinating internal retrieval of records;
- approving or escalating response decisions; and
- ensuring that request registers and evidence records are maintained.

## 6. How requests should be submitted
Requests should be submitted in writing to the Information Officer using the designated contact details.

The requester should provide enough information to allow the organisation to:
- identify the requester and verify their authority;
- identify the record or personal information requested;
- understand the scope and purpose of the request where legally relevant;
- contact the requester about the request;
- determine whether any fee, exception, or refusal ground applies; and
- keep a full record of the request lifecycle.

Where the Information Regulator prescribes a form for a particular type of request, Omni Logistics may ask the requester to complete that form or provide substantially similar information.

## 8. Records that may be automatically available
The final version of this manual should identify records that may be made available without a formal PAIA request, such as:
- public company information where publication is appropriate;
- public privacy notices;
- public access-to-information guidance;
- high-level product, marketing, and contact information; and
- other documents intentionally made public by the company.

## 9. Request handling process
The working request process is:
1. Receive and log the request.
2. Acknowledge receipt.
3. Verify identity and authority.
4. Clarify the scope of the request if needed.
5. Locate potentially responsive records.
6. Assess access, limitation, refusal, redaction, or third-party considerations.
7. Record the decision and response basis.
8. Provide the response and close the register item.

## 10. Request fees and cost handling
The final approved manual should state:
- whether request fees apply;
- when access fees may apply;
- how fee notices are communicated; and
- how exemptions or special handling will be considered.

This draft does not yet set fees and should not be treated as the final fee schedule.

## 11. Grounds for refusal, limitation, or redaction
The final approved manual should summarise the lawful grounds on which access may be refused, deferred, limited, or partially granted, including the treatment of:
- confidential commercial information;
- third-party information;
- legally privileged material;
- security-sensitive information;
- information whose disclosure is otherwise restricted by law; and
- records that do not exist or cannot reasonably be found after documented effort.

## 12. Personal information and POPIA-linked requests
Where requests relate to personal information, the handling process should also align with the company’s privacy notice, POPIA responsibilities, and internal subject-rights procedure.

## 13. Internal responsibilities
- Information Officer: final oversight and approval of response path.
- Administration / operations: request logging, coordination, and record location.
- Technical team: retrieval of system records, access logs, and operational evidence where needed.
- Finance / commercial owners: retrieval of invoices, billing, and commercial records.

## 14. Registers and evidence to maintain
At minimum, the following supporting records should be maintained:
- access request register;
- data subject request register;
- security incident register;
- processing activities register;
- version-controlled PAIA manual review history;
- evidence of approvals, training, and updates.

- after a serious incident or audit finding.
